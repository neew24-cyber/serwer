Privacy Policy

GDPR Compliance Statement

Your privacy is important to us. In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we have implemented stringent data protection measures to ensure the safety and privacy of the personal data we collect.

Personal Data We Collect

We collect and process personal data only when necessary. This might include, but is not limited to, your name, email address, and payment details. This data is primarily required for processing transactions, providing services, and for our internal analytics.

How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

- Process and manage your transactions.

- Enhance user experience and improve our website.

- Respond to inquiries and feedback.

- Send occasional newsletters or promotional materials (with your explicit consent).

Data Protection

We have implemented robust security measures to ensure your personal data is protected against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Furthermore, we restrict access to your personal data to those employees and partners who need access to fulfill their job responsibilities.

Your Rights

As per the GDPR, you have:

- The right to request access to, deletion of, or correction of your personal data.

- The right to oppose the processing, request data portability, and restrict the processing of your personal data.

- The right to withdraw consent.

Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies are small pieces of data that allow us to understand how users interact with our website. You have the right to block or restrict cookies from being stored on your device.

Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to external parties without your consent. This does not include trusted third parties necessary for our operations (like payment processors). These parties agree to keep this information confidential. We may also release your information when we believe it is appropriate to comply with the law or protect ours or others' rights, property, or safety.

Changes to This Policy

We may occasionally update our GDPR compliance statement. Any changes made will be posted on this page. We encourage users to periodically review our website for the latest information on our privacy practices.
