FAQ

  • How to get my vote reward?

    To claim your vote reward, simply vote for our server using the provided links. Then, log in to the game, head to the Community Board's 'Vote' tab, select the corresponding voting site, and collect your reward!

  • How to get Streamer's reward?

    To claim your Streamer reward, reach out to an Administrator to have your stream listed on our website. Your reward will be automatically granted based on your viewership and online time once your stream is added.

  • What is timezones?

    Discover our daily Timezones accessible through the sidebar buttons in-game. They reset daily at 06:00 GMT +2. Premium users enjoy a time multiplier of two. Additionally, we host a weekly instance that opens at specific times on designated days. These instances are shared across the same world for all players, creating opportunities for intense PvP and exciting interactions!

  • What is daily & weekly missions?

    Daily and weekly missions are fundamental to our server, forming the backbone of the gameplay experience. Completing these missions is essential, as they underpin the server's economy and progression. You'll receive your rewards automatically upon mission completion. Daily missions reset each day at 06:00, while weekly missions reset every Saturday at 06:00, ensuring a fresh and exciting challenge for players!
